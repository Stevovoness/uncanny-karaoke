Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Uncanny Karaoke ("we") is run by Barcor Industries. This policy explains what information our website and our posting tool handle. Contact: barcorindustries@gmail.com.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and doesn't collect any personal information. It's hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Our posting tool

We use our own software to post our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. It's used only by us, for the Uncanny Karaoke accounts. To do this, it connects to those platforms' APIs (including the YouTube API Services) with our permission, and handles:

  • Account details: our account's name and ID, to confirm which account a video is posted to.
  • Sign-in tokens: the access tokens the platforms issue when we sign in, so the tool can upload our videos. They're stored only on our own computer or server, never shared, and can be revoked at any time.
  • Posting results: the IDs and links of the videos we post.

We don't collect, store or process information about anyone else's account, and we don't sell, share or transfer any of this information to third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

YouTube and Google

Our tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Uncanny Karaoke's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings.

TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

Content we post there is also subject to the TikTok and Meta privacy policies. Access can be revoked in each platform's settings (connected or authorised apps).

Keeping and deleting information

We keep sign-in tokens only while the tool is connected, and delete them when we disconnect it. To ask about or request deletion of any information, email barcorindustries@gmail.com; we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Our content and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
